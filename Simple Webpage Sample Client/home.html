About Us

Lucky Shrub is a medium-sized garden design firm based in Tuscon, Arizona.
We specializes in garden design and creation, maintenance and landscaping.
Jason and Maria, founders and partners, shared a long-time love for plants. Jason is the garden architect––he creates and oversees all designs while managing his team of landscapers.
Maria manages all the marketing for the company and oversees the nursery.
